# Tiano Piles

Arcade piano tiles. Four lanes, tiles fall and speed up forever, one wrong tap and you're out. Tiles follow the real rhythm of Bach's Bourrée in E minor (BWV 996), melody and bass: an eighth note is one tile, a quarter is two tiles tall, a half note four. Saves your best score and best speed. No ads, works offline once installed.

**Play / install:** https://christiaanpbotha.github.io/tiano-piles/

- Android (Chrome): open the link → menu ⋮ → *Install app* / *Add to Home screen*
- iPhone (Safari): open the link → Share → *Add to Home Screen*

**Arpeggio mode:** a thumb-speed drill you can shape completely. Tap *Customize* on the start screen:

- Key, octave and mode (Ionian, Dorian, Phrygian, Lydian, Mixolydian, Aeolian, Locrian, harmonic and melodic minor, Phrygian dominant, double harmonic, whole tone)
- Any chord progression, as Roman numerals (`I vi v ii II II7 imaj7`, `bVII`, `V7/V`, `viiø7`, `IVadd#11`) or chord names (`Em7 Cmaj9 F#m11 Bb7/D`)
- Chords as written, or taken from the mode and stacked to triads, 7ths, 9ths, 11ths or 13ths
- Arpeggio direction, range (1-3 octaves), tiles per chord, bass on chord changes, sound (harpsichord, piano, soft, organ, bell)
- Lane pattern (sweep, left to right, right to left, follow the notes, random), start speed and speed-up
- *Hear it* plays the progression before you start; settings are remembered

Each mode keeps its own best score and best speed.

Keyboard: D F J K.
